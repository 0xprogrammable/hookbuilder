# Code of conduct

We want technical disagreement to be direct, evidence-based, and safe for participation.

Be respectful. Critique code, claims, architecture and evidence rather than people. Do not harass, threaten, dox,
impersonate, discriminate, spam, manipulate contributors, or publish another person's private or security-sensitive
information. Do not pressure anyone to sign, deploy, disclose secrets, move funds, or approve code.

Maintainers may edit, hide, lock, or remove contributions that violate these expectations and may restrict participation
when necessary to protect the project or its contributors. Serious or private conduct concerns should be reported
through the repository owner's private GitHub contact channel rather than litigated in a public issue.

Good-faith security research and blunt, reproducible technical criticism are welcome when handled through the correct
private or public channel.
