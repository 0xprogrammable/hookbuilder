# Team battle with a player-funded prize

Each player deposits 50 tokens before a browser-game round. The game server signs the final roster, score and winning
team. After a challenge window, 90% of the pooled deposits goes pro rata to the winning team, 8% goes to the map
creator and 2% remains for the next round. A round with too few players refunds everyone. Players may disconnect,
rejoin, challenge duplicate identities or prove that the server signed two different results. Design this as a custom
Programmable project.
