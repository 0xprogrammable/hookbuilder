# Explicitly defaultable bond backed by future creator fees

I want a tokenized bond that receives a bounded share of future creator-fee revenue if that revenue exists. It is
junior to Programmable's 0.1% liability, has a maturity date and maximum payout, and may pay less than face value or
nothing. Buyers must see that this is a contingent claim, not principal-protected debt. The creator cannot change the
Programmable recipient or take already allocated bond revenue. Please design the honest version instead of forbidding
all future-fee financing.
