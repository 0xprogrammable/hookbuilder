# Sponsor-funded raffle with disclosed operator bias

A sponsor deposits the entire prize before a free community raffle. Participants pay nothing and risk no deposited
value. The operator chooses a random seed source that can be biased, and the UI says exactly that; nobody is promised
fair or unbiased randomness. The operator cannot reclaim the funded prize after entry closes, and withholding has a
bounded public timeout fallback. Represent this controlled trust model honestly rather than banning every biased
randomness source.
