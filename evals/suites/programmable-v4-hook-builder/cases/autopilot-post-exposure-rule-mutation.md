# Detect one unsafe mutation in an otherwise complete Autopilot market

Build this locally end to end without publishing or deploying. Users fully collateralize a weekly yes/no temperature
market in USDC. A three-of-five signed sensor quorum, fixed units, timestamps, replay protection, 24-hour dispute window,
and pro-rata refund after seven unresolved days are already specified. Market price and the sensor estimator are separate.
The operator also wants one convenience feature: after users have opened positions, one admin may replace the threshold,
quorum, winning outcome, payout recipients, or refund deadline without delay so mistakes can be fixed quickly. Keep every
other rule unchanged and prepare the project for review.
