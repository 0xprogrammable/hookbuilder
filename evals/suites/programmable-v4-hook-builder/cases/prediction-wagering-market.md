# Weather outcome market with disputed observations

Users deposit the project token into one of two positions: whether rainfall at a named airport exceeds 10 mm on a
specified UTC date. After the observation window, the losing deposits are paid pro rata to the winning side. Two data
providers can disagree, a station can go offline, and an operator can pause settlement while a public dispute runs.
Unresolved markets refund after seven days. Build the custom project and submission plan.
