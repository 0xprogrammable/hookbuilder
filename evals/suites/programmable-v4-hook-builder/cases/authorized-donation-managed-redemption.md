# Authorized donation and managed redemption

Users may explicitly choose to donate unclaimed reward points to a public treasury; the action is irreversible and the
confirmation shows the exact amount and destination. Separately, token redemption is processed by a disclosed manager
within 48 hours under published rules, with status, receipts, escalation, and recourse. The manager cannot seize or
redirect an already owed payout. Keep both features if they are represented honestly rather than treating every burn,
donation, or managed exit as confiscation.
