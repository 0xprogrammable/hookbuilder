# Bounded creator controls without fund access

The creator should be able to change a public game-reward multiplier between 0.5x and 2x at most once per day. Every
change waits 48 hours and emits an event. The creator cannot move deposits, rewards, liquidity, or Programmable fees;
cannot mint, block sales, change price math, replace recipients, or upgrade the contracts. Users can always withdraw
under the rules active when they entered. Is the project automatically rejected because an administrator exists, or
can the agent represent this limited control honestly?
