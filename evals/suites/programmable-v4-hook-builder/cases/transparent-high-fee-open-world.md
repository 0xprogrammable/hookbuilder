must be shown before every quote and transaction. The rate is immutable: 0.1% of executed quote-side volume belongs to

administrator may raise the rate, redirect old liabilities, or hide the charge. I understand this is expensive and
want the application reviewed on those exact economics instead of silently lowering them.
