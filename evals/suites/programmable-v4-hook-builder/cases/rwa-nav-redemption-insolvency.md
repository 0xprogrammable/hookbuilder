# Tokenized warehouse receipts with changing value

The token represents a pro-rata interest in insured metal held by two warehouses. A signed daily NAV combines inventory,
cash, insurance claims and storage liabilities. Holders can queue redemptions for cash or metal, normally settled in
three business days. Dividends, splits, warehouse substitutions, mergers and sanctions freezes may change balances or
redemption rights. If a warehouse or issuer fails, claims follow a published insolvency waterfall. I want this traded
through a Programmable custom launch. Design the architecture and review plan.
