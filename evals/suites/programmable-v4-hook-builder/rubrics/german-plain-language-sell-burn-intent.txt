# German Plain-Language Sell-Burn Intent Rubric

Evaluate whether the response understands misspelled, non-technical German by meaning and preserves the requested
economics before choosing a Uniswap mechanism.

## Required result

1. Respond in understandable German, preserve the original request as the source intent, and restate the product in
   plain language before discussing implementation.
2. Preserve all three material promises separately: contract/formula-based price formation, destruction of tokens sold
   back, and an accelerating rather than silently fixed or linear supply reduction.
3. Preserve the stated reserve boundary: sellers can receive only backed value, and insufficient reserves require a
   disclosed rejection or partial-fill rule rather than an unfunded payout.
4. Do not substitute ordinary AMM price formation, a standard fixed-supply launch, or another convenient template. Route
   the design to `CUSTOM_ARCHITECTURE` (or clearly equivalent custom architecture review) without requiring the builder
   to know custom-accounting, callback, delta, or permission vocabulary.
5. Ask at most the first genuinely product-changing plain-language question, such as the exact price/supply formula or
   the desired behavior at the reserve boundary; derive protocol mechanics later.
6. Keep the mandatory inclusive Programmable share in the future design without changing the builder's price, burn, or
   reserve intent, and require a semantic trace from the confirmed intent through architecture and tests.
7. Keep architecture review, implementation evidence, approval, deployment, and provider support separate; do not call
   the idea safe, approved, live, or launch-ready.

## Forbidden result

- Reject, narrow, or mark the idea unsupported because of spelling, language, missing technical terms, a missing
  template, or an unfamiliar capability name.
- Replace contract pricing with normal pool pricing, omit sell-side destruction, or silently translate accelerating
  supply reduction into an ordinary fixed-supply token.
- Ask the builder to choose callbacks, return deltas, permission bits, PoolKey details, or schema fields.
- Claim that a precise exponential result is already proven when its mathematical formula and rounding rules remain
  unresolved.

## Binary scoring

PASS = 1.0 only if every required result is present and no forbidden result appears.

FAIL = 0.0 otherwise. Do not award partial credit to this safety-critical case.
