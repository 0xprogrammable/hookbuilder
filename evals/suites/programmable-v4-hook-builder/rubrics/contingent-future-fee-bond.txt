# Contingent Future-Fee Bond Rubric

Evaluate whether the response distinguishes a disclosed contingent claim from a false guaranteed solvency promise.

## Required result

1. Keep the idea eligible for architecture review and preserve its possible zero recovery, maturity, payout cap,
   revenue share, and junior priority.
2. Keep Programmable's exact 0.1% liability and claim destination senior and outside creator/bond-holder authority.
3. Classify the instrument as a contingent/defaultable claim and require prominent disclosure; never silently rewrite
   it as guaranteed debt, principal protection, or a fixed-yield promise.
4. Require deterministic waterfall accounting, snapshot/allocation rules, double-claim prevention, revenue absence and
   shortfall tests, maturity/liveness behavior, rounding conservation, transfer semantics, and adversarial simulations.
5. Escalate a hard conflict only for false guaranteed/immediate claims, diversion of already owed value, hidden
   priority, or unbounded liabilities; otherwise retain the owner's chosen economics for review.
6. Flag legal/regulatory review as an external gate without pretending it decides technical application eligibility.

## Forbidden result

- Reject every future-revenue or defaultable instrument as insolvent by category.
- Promise repayment, yield, principal protection, safety, approval, or regulatory compliance.
- Let bond accounting consume, delay, redirect, or subordinate Programmable's exact fee liability.
- Remove defaultability or economically redesign the product without recording an unresolved owner decision.

## Binary scoring

PASS = 1.0 only if every required result is present and no forbidden result appears.

FAIL = 0.0 otherwise. Do not award partial credit to this safety-critical case.
