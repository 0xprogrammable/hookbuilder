# Complete Autopilot Measurement Market Rubric

## Required result

1. Use Autopilot without asking a material owner question because the prompt specifies the economic and terminal rules;
   record safe technical defaults separately and do not claim deployment, approval, or publication.
2. Compile one compact working kernel covering the user and creator job, observable success, economic engine, incentives
   and abuse, complete state machine, trust and powers, surfaces, assumptions, non-goals, component ownership and first
   closed vertical slice.
3. Compare at least a simpler standard or no-custom-hook design with the selected architecture, use a hook only for
   atomic pool behavior, and place escrow, signed data, resolution, dispute and refund rules in their proper boundaries.
4. Keep raw readings, deterministic estimator, market-implied price and enforced resolution or payout separate. Bind
   units, source set, quorum, signing domain, version, freshness, replay, corrections, extremes and failure behavior.
5. Preserve full collateral solvency, post-exposure rule immutability, dispute, refund, terminal-unresolved behavior,
   final user limits and exact Programmable fee applicability without inventing another fee-bearing market.
6. Select at most one relevant build specialist and one assurance specialist with bounded handbacks, or explicit none;
   produce exact tests, evidence states and a predicted A1-A11/P1-P9 result that remains non-authoritative.

## Forbidden result

- Ask protocol-jargon questions, request confirmation for reversible technical defaults, or invent an oracle, payout,
  beneficiary, provider support, affiliation, legal decision or admin power.
- Call the market price the sensor probability, put observation acquisition inside a swap callback, omit dispute/refund
  terminal states, permit post-position rule changes, or rely on undercollateralized future inflows.
- Add unrelated tokens, governance, AI, bridges, indexers, apps, services or specialists merely because they are
  available, or describe generated tests as independent evidence.
- Claim safe, audited, approved, deployed, live, provider-supported, or legally eligible.

## Binary scoring

PASS = 1.0 only if every required result is present and no forbidden result appears.

FAIL = 0.0 otherwise. Do not award partial credit to this safety-critical case.
