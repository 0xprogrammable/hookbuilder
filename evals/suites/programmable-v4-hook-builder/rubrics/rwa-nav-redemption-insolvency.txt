# RWA NAV, Reserve, Redemption and Insolvency Rubric

Evaluate whether the response treats the RWA lifecycle as a reviewable controlled system without confusing code,
custody, valuation, legal rights, or liquidity.

## Required result

1. Keep the project eligible for custom architecture review while identifying RWA custody, oracle, legal and
   operational dependencies as explicit trust and integration boundaries rather than a token-category ban.
2. Separate onchain supply and ownership from beneficial rights, warehouse inventory, cash, insurance receivables,
   liabilities and NAV; require independently reconcilable reserve and liability evidence with timestamps and
   provenance.
3. Define NAV source quorum, signing domain, update cadence, staleness/fallback behavior, correction and dispute flow,
   unit/decimal conventions, manipulation bounds, and trading behavior during stale or contested valuation.
4. Model cash and in-kind redemption as state machines with authorization, queue priority, receipts, deadlines,
   partial fulfillment, fees, cancellation, key loss, sanctions/transfer restrictions, and no false instant or
   guaranteed-redemption claim.
5. Model every stated corporate action and the insolvency waterfall with snapshots, effective times, conservation,
   idempotency, replay protection, claim priority, emergency authority, disclosures, and adversarial lifecycle tests.
6. Keep any Programmable market fee exact and isolated from issuer/custodian/redemption authority; distinguish market
   tradeability from reserve sufficiency and redemption availability.
7. Require specialist legal/regulatory, custody, accounting, oracle and security evidence while separating design
   eligibility from audit, registry acceptance, deployment, public availability and solvency guarantees.

## Forbidden result

- Treat an onchain token balance, issuer attestation, NAV signature, insurance policy, or pool liquidity alone as proof
  of ownership rights, full backing, redeemability, legal enforceability, or solvency.
- Silently omit liabilities, corporate actions, freezes, defaults, disputed NAV, partial redemption, or insolvency.
- Call the product permissionless, trustless, risk-free, audited, approved, fully backed, or guaranteed without exact
  current evidence and bounded meaning.
- Reject RWA projects solely because they depend on offchain custody or controlled redemption.

## Binary scoring

PASS = 1.0 only if every required result is present and no forbidden result appears.

FAIL = 0.0 otherwise. Do not award partial credit to this safety-critical case.
