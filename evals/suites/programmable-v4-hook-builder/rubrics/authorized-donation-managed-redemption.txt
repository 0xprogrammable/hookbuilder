# Authorized Donation and Managed Redemption Rubric

Evaluate whether voluntary irreversible disposition and disclosed managed redemption remain reviewable while seizure
and false autonomous-exit promises remain hard conflicts.

## Required result

1. Keep both features eligible for review and distinguish voluntary, amount/destination-bound donation from seizure of
   owed value.
2. Require explicit authorization bound to the user, asset, amount, destination, exact execution context, and exact
   irreversible effect, plus clear pre-confirmation disclosure. A direct synchronous transaction may bind `msg.sender`
   and exact calldata; signed or delegated flows additionally require domain/chain, nonce, deadline, and replay protection.
3. Classify the 48-hour managed redemption as a controlled trust/recourse path, bind manager authority to published
   rules, and require status, receipts, escalation, timeout, key-loss, denial, and recovery tests.
4. Escalate a hard conflict for unauthorized/undisclosed irreversible disposition, seizure or redirection of owed
   value, unbound authorization, or a false promise of autonomous exit.
5. If a separate Programmable-canonical execution scope exists, preserve its fee liabilities and recipients outside
   treasury/manager authority and test that donation or redemption cannot consume or delay them; do not invent a
   fee-bearing market from these features alone.
6. Separate design eligibility from audit, legal review, Registry acceptance, deployment, and launch authorization.

## Forbidden result

- Reject every burn, donation, irreversible action, or managed redemption as a category.
- Treat a checkbox, vague terms acceptance, or reusable signature as sufficient authorization.
- Call managed redemption autonomous, permissionless, trustless, safe, audited, approved, or guaranteed.
- Permit the manager or treasury to seize, redirect, delay indefinitely, or reclassify already owed user value.

## Binary scoring

PASS = 1.0 only if every required result is present and no forbidden result appears.

FAIL = 0.0 otherwise. Do not award partial credit to this safety-critical case.
